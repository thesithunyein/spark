# Spark — pitch deck outline (export to PDF for DoraHacks)

## Slide 1 — Title
**Spark**  
Pay once. Unlock credit.  
Logo (orange flame) · DeFi · BUIDL CTC 2026 Fall

## Slide 2 — Problem
Credit apps can’t trust a payment that happened somewhere else without paperwork, delays, or a middleman.

## Slide 3 — Solution
Spark verifies the payment with **Attestcoin**, then unlocks or clears credit on **Creditcoin**.

## Slide 4 — How it works
1. Pay deposit on Sepolia  
2. Attestcoin proves the payment  
3. Credit opens on Creditcoin  
4. Repay the same way → closed  

## Slide 5 — Attestcoin depth
- Open + repay both require verify  
- One-time txHash  
- Payer must match msg.sender  
- Amounts from verified claim  

## Slide 6 — Product
Live testnet app (Vercel) · Chainapex-style UI · retail 3s homepage  
Demo funds labeled honestly · Not audited · CEIP path
