PROBLEM

Credit cannot trust a payment that happened somewhere else

Without paperwork, delays, or a middleman, most credit systems cannot verify that a deposit or repayment occurred on another chain.

  • Manual reconciliation is slow and opaque
  • Centralized oracles and bridges add trust risk
  • Cross-chain DeFi needs cryptographic proof, not receipts
Spark2 / 7

SOLUTION

Verify payment. Then open credit on Creditcoin.

Spark uses the Attestcoin Protocol (formerly USC) so a Sepolia payment can unlock or clear a credit line on Creditcoin without a centralized oracle.

  • Deposit on Sepolia → Attestcoin proves the tx → credit opens
  • Repay the same way → line closes
  • No valid proof means no state change

ATTESTCOIN PROTOCOL

Payment verification is required, not optional

  • openCredit and repayCredit call the verifier
  • One-time txHash (replay protected)
  • Payer must match msg.sender
  • Amounts come from the verified claim
